About Crony Electronics Pvt Ltd.

Crony Electronics Pvt Ltd is involved into energy saving products & activities since 1993. We have strived for performance & optimum output for our customers ever since. Energy saving product for Air conditioners were the first to be introduced by us in 1994.

As a mark of excellence in our field the company has grown leaps & bounds. This is proven by the fact that we cater to the likes of ONGC, Railways, L&T, M&M, Cipla, Kirloskar Brothers Ltd, Hindalco, Sesa Goa & so on. Our wings are spread across all the geographical regions of India.

Our continuous efforts as a team have resulted in first of its kind microprocessor based digital energy saving product for AC way back in 1997. The product still runs as a favourite in the scenario of changing technologies goes to prove its efficiency, reliability & performance for more than 2 Decades now

We are presently catering to around 500 corporate customers with this technology & the customer are ever increasing. Just goes to show the sort of confidence customers show in our products.

Our latest step towards energy management & improving the efficiency of the industries is HEAT reflective coating (Elastoclad HR) used on the exterior of rooftops, walls etc. This coating helps in reducing the surface to surface temperature by 10 to 12 degree Celsius. The thermography results of these coatings prove the efficiency & claim of product. Apart from reducing of surface to surface temperature this coat also acts as a waterproofing agent for the applied surfaces. The whole idea is to give multiple advantages with one product.

Innovation & savings has always aspired our range of products hence our latest product in the kitty is Removable Thermal Insulation Sleeves to aid the typical scenario of helping customers save on power as the product can take care of the most troubled areas like flanges, valves etc that require frequent servicing & maintenance making it extremely difficult to replace the conventional mode of insulation. Our latest removable thermal insulation sleeves are a perfect solution for these troubled areas. These sleeves could be removed & re installed by any one more over helps save a Huge amount of energy cost & inventory cost. The product is custom built to fit any shape & size of unit.

Our sincere effort is to give multiple utility with our products & maximize the profits for our customers. We pledge to give the best & involve ourselves in many new innovations to come to optimize the safety energy efficiency of our customers at minimum cost.
