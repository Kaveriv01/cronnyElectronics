About Crony Electronics Pvt Ltd.

Over 3 Decades of Excellence in Industrial Energy Management

Pioneering Energy Saving Since 1993

Crony Electronics Pvt Ltd is involved into energy saving products & activities since 1993. We have strived for performance & optimum output for our customers ever since. Energy saving products for Air conditioners were the first to be introduced by us in 1994.

As a mark of excellence in our field the company has grown leaps & bounds. This is proven by the fact that we cater to the likes of ONGC, Indian Railways, L&T, Mahindra & Mahindra, Cipla, Kirloskar Brothers Ltd, Hindalco, Sesa Goa & so on. Our wings are spread across all the geographical regions of India.

Our continuous efforts as a team have resulted in first of its kind microprocessor based digital energy saving product for AC way back in 1997. The product still runs as a favourite in the scenario of changing technologies goes to prove its efficiency, reliability & performance for more than 2 Decades now.

We are presently catering to around 500 corporate customers with this technology & the customers are ever increasing. Just goes to show the sort of confidence customers show in our products.

Our Aim

“To maximize the safety & savings of our clients on power and stay dedicated & determined to give the best. We pledge to give the best & involve ourselves in many new innovations to optimize safety and energy efficiency of our customers at minimum cost.”

Our latest step towards energy management & improving the efficiency of industries is HEAT reflective coating (Elastoclad HR) used on the exterior of rooftops, walls etc. This coating helps in reducing surface-to-surface temperature by 10 to 12 degrees Celsius, certified by thermography test results, while doubling as a heavy-duty waterproofing agent.

Innovation & savings has always inspired our range of products. Hence our latest product in the kitty is Removable Thermal Insulation Sleeves / Jackets to aid troubled areas like flanges, valves, and pipes that require frequent maintenance, replacing conventional insulation with custom-built removable covers that save huge energy and inventory costs.

Quality Assurances & GMP Compliance

We give complete quality assurances for all our products through our stringent quality checks. Various electrical testings are done to check the workability of the products. The products are also tested for robust structure, durability, reliability, accuracy, raw materials etc. All these tests are conducted by our quality controllers who keep a strict vigil on the entire production stages. We also make it a point to adhere to relevant quality norms and Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) that add to our company's credibility in the market. The presence of modern gadgetry to test the products enables us to check them for accuracy and quality. These tests also ensure a trouble-free, long, and dependable operation minus the drawbacks.

End-to-End Solutions

Comprehensive Product Range

Our all-inclusive assortment comprises high-efficiency thermal barriers, microprocessor energy savers, heavy-duty industrial floor coverings, and precision voltage regulators:

Elastoclad HR Heat Coating

Advanced heat-reflective elastomeric coating for rooftops and exterior industrial walls. Reduces surface temperatures by 10 to 12°C with certified thermography proof while providing full waterproofing.

Removable Thermal Jackets

Custom-built thermal insulation sleeves and jackets for flanges, valves, pumps, and pipes. Replaces messy conventional insulation, allows easy removal for maintenance, and saves immense energy cost.

Digi Guard & Eco Max AC Savers

Microprocessor-based digital energy-saving controllers for air conditioners. Pioneered by Crony Electronics in 1997, delivering proven reliability, power savings, and compressor longevity for over two decades.

Servo & Fast Voltage Regulators

High-speed voltage regulation and servo stabilization protecting sensitive industrial automation, robotics, and mission-critical production machinery against sags, spikes, and brownouts.

Heavy-Duty Rubber Flooring

norament® and noraplan® industrial rubber floor coverings withstanding 6 N/mm² dynamic forklift loads, 10-year ESD warranty, and ISO Class 3 / GMP Grade A cleanroom certified surfaces.

Industrial Robotics & Automation

Engineered automation solutions comprising 64 specialized robotic units for high-precision industrial manufacturing, assembly lines, palletizing, and hazardous material handling.
