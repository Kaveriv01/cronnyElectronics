ABOUT CRONY ELECTRONICS

30+ Years of Energy Saving Excellence

Pioneering Industrial Thermal & Energy Management Since 1993

For over three decades, Crony Electronics Pvt Ltd has engineered proven energy-saving and asset protection solutions. First introducing dedicated AC energy systems in 1994 and microprocessor digital controllers in 1997 with Eata Electronics, we help commercial facilities and industrial plants achieve verified reductions in electricity consumption.

  • Microprocessor AC Optimization: Co-developed digital controllers regulating HVAC compressors to cut power draw by 15% to 30% with zero cooling loss.
  • Elastoclad Heat-Reflective Roof Barrier: Blocks up to 85% of solar infrared rays, reducing building surface temperatures by 10°C–12°C and lowering HVAC load.
  • 20ms Solid-State Voltage Regulation (VRP): One of the world's fastest solid-state stabilizers (3 to 360 kVA), safeguarding sensitive automation, CNCs, and electronics.

Microprocessor AC Digital Energy Savers (Since 1997)

Our continuous efforts as a team with Eata Electronics resulted in first of its kind microprocessor based digital energy saving product for AC way back in 1997. The product still runs as a favourite in the scenario of changing technologies goes to prove its efficiency, reliability & performance for more than 2 Decades now. We are presently catering to around 500 corporate customers with this technology & the customers are ever increasing.

Elastoclad HR & Glass IR Blocking Coatings

Our latest step towards energy management is HEAT reflective coating (Elastoclad HR) on rooftops and exterior walls, reducing surface-to-surface temperature by 10 to 12°C with certified thermography results and dual waterproofing benefits. Furthermore, our IR blocking transparent coating for glass facades blocks up to 95% of IR rays, curtailing ambient heat while ensuring clear visual transparency.

Removable Thermal Insulation Sleeves

Custom-built removable thermal insulation sleeves engineered specifically for troubled areas like flanges, valves, pumps, and pipes that require frequent maintenance. Traditional insulation is difficult and expensive to replace; our removable sleeves can be effortlessly removed and re-installed by anyone, saving huge energy costs and inventory expenses.

VRP Voltage Regulators, Brilantor Daylight & 64 Robots

Sensitive equipment demands flawless power conditions. Our VRP (Voltage Regulation with Precision) solid-state units regulate voltage dips and rises in just 20 milliseconds — one of the fastest in the world (3 KVA to 360 KVA). We also deliver Brilantor Natural Daylight providing up to 400 lux without power or heat, plus up to 64 specialized robotic automation models.
