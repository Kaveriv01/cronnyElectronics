We have 30+ years of industrial experience

Crony Electronics Pvt Ltd is involved into energy saving products & activities since 1993. We have strived for performance & optimum output for our customers ever since. Energy saving products for Air conditioners were first introduced by us in 1994, followed by the first of its kind microprocessor-based digital energy saving product for AC in 1997. The product still runs as a favourite across changing technologies for more than two decades, proving its efficiency, reliability & performance. We cater to around 500 corporate customers including ONGC, Indian Railways, L&T, M&M, Cipla, Kirloskar Brothers Ltd, and Hindalco across all geographical regions of India.
