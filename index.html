Industrial & Energy Product Portfolio

Advanced Solutions for Heavy Industry & Energy Efficiency

Rubber Flooring (Industry & Trade)

norament® & noraplan® heavy-duty rubber floor coverings from nora systems GmbH. Withstands 6 N/mm² dynamic forklift load, 10-year ESD warranty, and ISO Class 3 / GMP Grade A cleanrooms with zero coatings needed.

Elastoclad HR & IR Glass Shield

Elastoclad HR HEAT reflective exterior coating drops rooftop surface temperatures by 10 to 12°C with thermography verification and waterproofing. IR transparent coatings block 95% IR heat through glass façades.

Thermal Insulation Jackets & Sleeves

Custom removable insulation jackets for pipeline valves, flanges, pumps, and exhaust manifolds. Cuts heat loss up to 90%, prevents personnel burn injuries, and withstands continuous heat up to 600°C.

20ms High-Speed VRP Voltage Regulators

Solid-state precision voltage regulation (3 kVA to 360 kVA). Corrects severe voltage sags and spikes in sub-20 milliseconds to safeguard sensitive CNCs, automation lines, and microprocessors from downtime.

DigiGuard EcoMax AC Energy Savers

Pioneering microprocessor-based digital energy saver for commercial air conditioners since 1997. Modulates compressor duty cycles based on thermodynamic saturation, reducing 15% to 30% kWh across 500+ clients.

Brilantor Natural Daylight Harvesting

Advanced optical skylighting system delivering up to 400 lux natural, glare-free daylight into industrial factory sheds for 10 hours daily. Zero electricity consumption with 99% UV radiation block.

Industrial Autonomous Robotics (64 Types)

Deploying up to 64 specialized robotic solutions: heavy-duty Nilfisk factory floor scrubbers, autonomous solar panel cleaners, AGV lifters, and sanitation robots to lower operational overhead.
