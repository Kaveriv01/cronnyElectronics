Common Inquiries & Technical Support

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Elastoclad HR and how does it reduce energy consumption?

Elastoclad HR is a 100% acrylic waterproofing and solar-reflective coating formulated with hollow ceramic microspheres. It dissipates and reflects up to 85% of solar radiation, resulting in a proven 8 to 12°C surface temperature reduction and significantly lowering air conditioning energy consumption.

Where can Elastoclad HR heat-reflective coating be applied?

Elastoclad HR can be applied using brush, roller, or airless spray on industrial metal roof decks, silos, storage tanks, electrical panels, cold storage enclosures, and concrete roofs and exterior walls.

How do 20ms VRP Voltage Regulators protect industrial equipment?

Our VRP Solid State Voltage Regulators feature an ultra-fast 20-millisecond response time without any moving mechanical parts. They provide instantaneous correction against sags, surges, and spikes, protecting sensitive CNC robotics, PLC controls, and automation lines from costly downtime.

Why choose nora® industrial rubber floorings for demanding facilities?

Manufactured in Germany, nora® rubber floorings offer extraordinary dimensional stability that never requires joint welding, withstands extreme heavy forklift and AGV loads, provides certified ESD protection, and contains zero PVC or harmful plasticizers.
