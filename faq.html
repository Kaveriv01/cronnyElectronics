Technical & Commercial FAQs

How does Elastoclad HR heat-reflective coating reduce surface temperatures by 10 to 12°C?

Elastoclad HR is formulated with high-grade elastomeric polymers and specialized ceramic microspheres that reflect up to 90% of solar radiation. On-site thermography test results confirm a direct surface temperature drop of 10°C to 12°C on metal roofing and exterior concrete walls. This drastically cuts heat transfer into the building, reducing HVAC energy consumption by up to 25% while simultaneously acting as a heavy-duty waterproofing agent.

What makes Removable Thermal Insulation Jackets superior to conventional cladding?

Conventional metal cladding or fiberglass insulation on valves, flanges, pumps, and pipe joints is difficult to remove during routine maintenance and often gets damaged or discarded, leaving hot surfaces bare. Crony's removable thermal jackets are custom-tailored with high-temperature industrial fabrics and quick-release fasteners. They can be removed and re-installed within minutes by anyone, eliminating inventory replacement costs and preventing immense ongoing thermal energy waste.

How do Digi Guard & Eco Max microprocessors save power on industrial air conditioning?

Pioneered by Crony Electronics in 1997, Digi Guard and Eco Max continuously monitor ambient room temperature and cooling coil thermodynamic saturation. They intelligently cycle the compressor to eliminate dry running and thermodynamic over-saturation, delivering 15% to 30% electrical energy savings while significantly extending compressor lifespan.

What dynamic load and cleanroom certifications does nora® rubber flooring support?

Supplied in partnership with nora systems GmbH, norament® and noraplan® floor coverings withstand high dynamic loads up to 6 N/mm² (including high-tonnage forklifts). They require zero wax or chemical coatings, offer a 10-year ESD warranty, and are certified for ISO Class 3 and GMP Grade A cleanrooms with zero VOC emissions.

How fast is the 20ms VRP Voltage Regulator compared to traditional servo stabilizers?

Traditional motor-driven servo stabilizers take hundreds of milliseconds to compensate for voltage sags and surges, which is too slow to protect modern robotics, CNCs, and microprocessors. The 20ms Voltage Regulating Processor (VRP) responds in under 20 milliseconds, providing instantaneous voltage correction, brownout protection, and pure sine wave output.

Does Crony Electronics offer pan-India site audits and on-time implementation?

Yes. Since 1993, Crony Electronics has served over 500 corporate clients across all geographical regions of India (including ONGC, Indian Railways, L&T, Mahindra, Cipla, and Kirloskar). Our technicians conduct on-site thermal profiling, dimensional measurement, and technical consultation, backed by capacious warehousing and on-time pan-India delivery.
